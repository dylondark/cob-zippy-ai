# University of Akron College of Business - Knowledge Base

## General Information
The College of Business at the University of Akron offers undergraduate and graduate programs in business administration, accounting, finance, marketing, and management.

## Programs
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
- Master of Business Administration (MBA)
- Master of Accountancy (MAcc)
- Ph.D. in Business Administration

## Location
The College of Business is located on the main campus of the University of Akron in Akron, Ohio.

## Accreditation
The College of Business is accredited by AACSB International (The Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business).
